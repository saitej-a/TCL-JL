<objective>
Expose 7.1's read-only analytics service over HTTP and make it cheap to serve, satisfying
ANAL-01, ANAL-02 and ANAL-05 (roadmap plan 07-02, MVP tasks T7.3–T7.10).

The phase adds the four `/api/v1/analytics/*` endpoints plus 04 §80's `/api/v1/public/stats/`,
a payload-level Redis cache behind the hourly Celery Beat warmup already reserved in
`config/celery.py`, per-row privacy suppression, an anonymous read throttle, whitelist filter
validation, and both wait-time baselines (survey→JL and offer→JL) inside the overview payload.

Two deliberate changes to 7.1's shipped contract: the `<5` floor moves from the slice total to
each row (D-01), and the suppressed payload's four-key shape is preserved while non-suppressed
payloads gain `generated_at` and a `wait_times` block (additive).
</objective>
