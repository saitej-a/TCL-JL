## Areas Explored & Decisions Made

### 1. Wait-Time Baseline & Fallbacks (ANAL-03)
- **Choice:** Offer/Interview to JL.
- **Preference:** `OFFER_LETTER -> JOINING_LETTER` is primary. If Offer Letter date is missing, fallback to `INTERVIEW -> JOINING_LETTER`.
- **Source Priority:** Check `TimelineEvent` records first; if absent, fall back to `CandidateProfile.offer_letter_date` or `CandidateProfile.interview_date`.
- **Stats:** Return Mean, Median, Min, Max, and Sample Size.

### 2. Privacy Suppression Enforcement (ANAL-04)
- **Threshold:** `ANALYTICS_MIN_COHORT_SIZE = 5`.
- **Scope:** Full Cohort Suppression. If the cohort or filtered query has `< 5` candidates, suppress the entire sub-breakdown to prevent micro-cohort re-identification.
- **Payload:** Return `{"data_source": "COMMUNITY_REPORTED", "suppressed": true, "message": "..."}`.

### 3. App Architecture
- Read-only service layer (`apps/analytics/services.py`).
- Zero database write models, zero migrations.
- Provenance label `COMMUNITY_REPORTED` and official non-affiliation disclaimer attached to all service outputs.
