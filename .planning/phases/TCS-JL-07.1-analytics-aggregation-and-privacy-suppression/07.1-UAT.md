## Tests

### 1. The <5 rule suppresses every aggregation surface, and never partially
expected: Run `docker compose exec -T web python .planning/phases/TCS-JL-07.1-analytics-aggregation-and-privacy-suppression/drill_07_01.py`. A 2-candidate cohort is refused by all four aggregation helpers with the standard message and no counts, no zeroed buckets and no empty results list; the suppressed payload carries exactly four keys; the drill passes 16/16 and restores the database to 21/21 candidates and 66/66 events.
result: [pending]

### 2. The floor is a setting, not a hardcoded 5
expected: `pytest apps/analytics/tests/test_privacy_suppression.py -v` shows `test_suppression_threshold_dynamically_respects_settings` passing: with `ANALYTICS_MIN_COHORT_SIZE` overridden to 3 at runtime, a 3-candidate cohort is served instead of suppressed, while a 4-candidate cohort is still suppressed under the default 5. Nothing about the threshold needs a migration or a restart to change.
result: [pending]

### 3. Wait times follow the full fallback chain and produce the numbers a human would compute
expected: Reading the drill output, the 6-candidate cohort that has a calculable wait reports `average_days: 21.7`, `median_days: 20.0`, `min_days: 10`, `max_days: 40`, `sample_size: 6` — matching the raw seeded dates (two 10-day waits from an OFFER_LETTER event, two 20-day waits from an INTERVIEW event, one 30-day wait from the profile's own offer_letter_date, one 40-day wait from interview_date). `pytest apps/analytics/tests/test_wait_time_calculations.py -v` shows all seven cases passing, including `test_wait_time_prefers_offer_event_over_interview_event` and `test_wait_time_fallback_to_profile_dates`.
result: [pending]

### 4. A candidate whose joining letter predates their baseline is left out, never clamped
expected: `pytest apps/analytics/tests/test_wait_time_calculations.py -v` shows `test_wait_time_excludes_negative_intervals` passing: the drill seeds a corrupted candidate whose JOINING_LETTER is dated 50 days before their OFFER_LETTER, and the sample stays at 6 with min 10 / max 40 rather than absorbing a -50. No clamp, no absolute value, no silent 0.
result: [pending]
