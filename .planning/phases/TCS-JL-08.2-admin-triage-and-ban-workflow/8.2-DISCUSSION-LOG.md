## Claude's Discretion

- LOCK_POST restricted to post-targeted reports (400 on comment targets) — follows §4.2.3's post-only effect without needing a user call.
- REMOVE_CONTENT on already-tombstoned targets stays idempotent (re-flag + resolve) — consistent with 8.1's tombstone-inclusive reporting semantics.
- Ban from a review acts on the report's content author; the standalone endpoint takes the user id — both share `moderation.services.ban_user` (D5).
- REST review/ban responses use 202 for the Celery-followed paths (D1's async consequence); DISMISS resolves synchronously with 200.
